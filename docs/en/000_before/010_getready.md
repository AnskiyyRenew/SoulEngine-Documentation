We highly recommend *people with some Lua foundation come to learn*.

But we do not reject newcomers either.

---

## Hardware Requirements

Through continuous testing, we found that even a low-spec machine with an `i3` can run `Soul Engine` without any problem, so you don't need to worry about the engine blowing up your computer.

A minimum of `4G` of RAM is sufficient.

---

## Operating System Support

Apart from the hardware requirements mentioned above, some operations in `Soul Engine` cannot function properly on systems other than `Windows`. This will be highlighted with warning boxes in later documentation.

---

## Tech Stack Overview

- `Lua` will be the primary development language.
- `GLSL` will be used later when writing `Shaders`. Don't learn it for now; if interested, you can check the `Shader Development` topic. But if you're a complete beginner—don't look.

If you want to get a quick feel for how `Lua` is written and avoid pitfalls later, we recommend the [tutorial](./020_lua.md) in the docs.

---

## Math Requirements

These contents are not strictly mandatory—I'm not saying you don't need to learn them; on the contrary, <u>being able to apply them proficiently is the ultimate goal</u>.

In the early stages of development, we won't use much math knowledge.

- `Arithmetic`—addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division; these are the most commonly used.
- `Trigonometry`—the module that requires the most mathematical foundation.
- `Basic middle-school math`—sounds fancy, but it's really just things like `absolute value`.

**The above are the most basic mathematical frameworks used in game development.**

If you want more advanced or challenging math knowledge, please go to the `Math` topic for more content.

---

## Patience Requirements

No matter how fast you learn, you can't master everything in a single day, so calm down and follow the documentation step by step.
